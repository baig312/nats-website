Services

01

Complete Turnkey Fit-Out

From design and civil works to final handover.

One contract from the first drawing to the day you move in. Design, civil works, MEP, joinery and finishes are delivered by a single team on one programme and one accountable price — so there are no gaps between trades and no one else to chase.

  • —Design & technical drawings
  • —Civil & structural works
  • —Authority approvals & permits
  • —Flooring, ceilings & partitions
  • —Joinery, finishes & FF&E
  • —Snagging & final handover
02

Landscaping & Swimming Pools

Complete design, construction and maintenance.

Outdoor spaces designed, built and cared for as one. Pools, gardens, irrigation and lighting are planned alongside the villa, engineered for the Gulf climate, and maintained so they stay as good as the day they were handed over.

  • —Landscape & garden design
  • —Hardscape, decking & pergolas
  • —Custom pools & water features
  • —Filtration, heating & dosing
  • —Irrigation & outdoor lighting
  • —Pool & garden maintenance
03

Home Automation & Home Cinema

Smart living and entertainment, fully integrated.

We design, install and integrate the systems that make a home effortless — lighting, climate, security and audio-visual on one intuitive platform — through to dedicated cinema rooms tuned for picture, sound and comfort.

  • —Lighting & scene control
  • —Climate, blinds & energy
  • —Security, CCTV & access
  • —Multi-room audio & video
  • —Dedicated home cinema rooms
  • —Single-app & voice control
04

MEP Services

Mechanical, electrical & plumbing — designed, installed, maintained.

The systems behind every finished space. Our in-house MEP team designs, installs and maintains mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, coordinated with the fit-out so services are right first time — and easy to service for years after.

  • —HVAC & ventilation
  • —Electrical power & lighting
  • —Plumbing & drainage
  • —Water heating & pumps
  • —Low-current & data cabling
  • —Testing & commissioning
05

Interior Design & Space Planning

Concept, 3D visualisation and full execution.

From first idea to the finished room. We plan the space, develop the concept, show it to you in photorealistic 3D — then build it ourselves, so the design you approve is exactly the one that gets delivered.

  • —Space planning & layouts
  • —Concept & mood boards
  • —Photorealistic 3D visualisation
  • —Material & finish selection
  • —Bespoke joinery & lighting design
  • —Full execution on site
06

Facilities Management & AMC

Annual maintenance for fit-out, MEP and automation.

Care that continues after handover. Our annual maintenance contracts keep your interiors, MEP and smart systems performing at their best — with scheduled preventive visits, prompt response when something needs attention, and a team that already knows your property.

  • —Annual maintenance contracts
  • —Planned preventive maintenance
  • —HVAC & MEP servicing
  • —Automation & AV support
  • —Minor works & repairs
  • —Responsive call-out support
