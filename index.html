Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp — we usually reply within the hour.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Al Karama, Dubai and work on villas, apartments and commercial spaces across Dubai and the wider UAE.

Do you really handle everything under one contract?

Yes. Fit-out, MEP, interior design, home automation & cinema, swimming pools, landscaping and ongoing maintenance are all delivered by NATS on one licence — so you deal with a single project lead, not five subcontractors.

Is the first site visit really free?

It is. We visit the space, understand the brief and give you an honest view on budget and timeline before anything is committed.

Do you maintain the project after handover?

Yes — we offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC) covering fit-out, MEP and automation systems, so the space keeps working long after it's finished.

How do I get an estimate?

Try our instant cost estimate for a ballpark, then book a free site visit for a precise, itemised quote.
