About us

Design. Build. Automate. Under one roof.

Naama Albayina Technical Services L.L.C (NATS) is a Dubai-based construction, fit-out and technical services company. We deliver complete turnkey projects for homes, offices and commercial spaces across the UAE — from design and civil works to smart automation and long-term maintenance, all from one team under one contract.

Our story

Built on a decade of construction experience.

NATS is led by its Owner and Managing Director, Muhammad Abdullah, whose background is in property development and construction in the United Kingdom and Pakistan. Since 2015, our team has delivered residential, commercial and development projects across both countries, taking them from ground-up construction through to finished interiors.

In 2023 we brought that experience to the UAE. We spent our first years here working on projects in partnership with established local companies — learning the market, building our trade teams and earning our clients' trust. In 2026 we launched under our own name and our own licence, so every project now carries our full accountability from start to finish.

The owner's vision

Clients in the UAE deserve one builder who owns the whole result — not a chain of contractors pointing at each other. My vision for NATS is to design, build and look after every space as if it were our own home.

Since 2015 I've seen how projects go wrong: when design, construction and systems are split between companies, the client pays for the gaps. NATS exists to close them. One accountable team, honest advice, a clear written scope, and a finished space we're proud to put our name on — then we stay with you after handover to keep it that way.

Our roots

Our roots go deeper than fit-out. That manufacturing background gives us a real understanding of materials, quality control and how buildings are put together — and it carries through into every project we deliver in the UAE.
